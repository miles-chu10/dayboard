# DayBoard 1.2.0

- Sign in with Google in your browser — no Google Cloud project, Client ID, or Client Secret needed. Settings → Sources now shows just Sign in with Google, Sign in again, and Sign out.
- Read emails without leaving DayBoard: open a message from Inbox or your Agenda to see the full body, mark it read, reply, archive, or open it in Gmail. It follows your Pop-up, Inline, or Side panel detail setting.
- Open Google Tasks and Apple Reminders from their own pages in the same detail view used on the Agenda.
- Add a profile picture from Settings → General or by clicking your profile in the sidebar; your initials show when no picture is set.
- A simpler sidebar: Agenda, Calendar, Inbox, Tasks, and Reminders, followed by one compact AI group with Assistant, Weekly Review, and MCP Servers. Up Next opens the event's details.
- Tighter page spacing and a cleaner Agenda title.
- Security: updated a build tool (esbuild) to fix a reported vulnerability.

Google sign-in currently works only for approved test accounts while DayBoard awaits Google's app verification.
