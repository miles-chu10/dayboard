Privacy policy

Privacy, plainly.

DayBoard keeps your calendar, tasks, reminders and mail between your Mac and the services you connect. This page explains what goes where. Last updated September 23, 2026.

The DayBoard app

  • No tracking. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and DayBoard has no server that receives your calendar, tasks, reminders or mail.
  • On your Mac: settings, linked items and Assistant history are stored locally. Google sign-in tokens are encrypted with macOS's built-in protection.
  • Google: when you sign in, DayBoard asks for access to Google Calendar, Google Tasks and, in the beta, Gmail. It talks to Google's APIs directly from your Mac to show and change those items for you. Signing out revokes DayBoard's access.
  • Apple Reminders: read and changed on your Mac through macOS, with your permission.
  • AI (optional): nothing goes to an AI provider until you choose one. When you use an AI feature, DayBoard sends the context that request needs, such as the items or message you ask about, to the provider you chose, through Claude Code or Codex on your Mac or with your API key. That provider handles it under its own terms. Dictation sends your recording to OpenAI for transcription, only when you use it.
  • MCP server (optional): off by default. When you turn it on, it runs only on your Mac, and clients need the access key shown in Settings.
  • Official builds: activating a license sends your license key, a random installation ID and the label "DayBoard on macOS" to DayBoard's license service. Checking for updates contacts GitHub. Beta builds do neither.

Google user data

DayBoard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

DayBoard uses Google data only to show and change your calendar, tasks and mail inside the app, and, when you ask, to send the relevant items to the AI provider you chose. It doesn't sell Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or train AI models.

This website

  • Beta signups: if you join the beta, we store your email address and the time you signed up in DayBoard's signup database. We use it only to send beta invites and major beta news, and we never sell or share it. We keep it until the beta ends or you ask us to delete it.
  • Abuse protection: to limit repeated signup attempts, the signup service keeps a keyed hash of your IP address, not the address itself.
  • No cookies or trackers: this site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Fonts and images are served from this site. Our hosting provider processes basic request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver the site and block abuse.

Removal and questions

To have your email removed, or to ask about this policy, email the DayBoard team. If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
