Join the DayBoard waitlist.

DayBoard is in early access. Join the waitlist to tell us you're interested, and we'll invite people in small groups as we're ready for more testers.

How the waitlist works

  • Joining is a request, not a reservation. It tells us you'd like to try DayBoard. It doesn't create an account or download anything.
  • Invitations go out in small groups, as DayBoard is ready for more testers. There's no set date, and joining doesn't guarantee an invitation.
  • If you're invited, we'll email this address with a download link and install steps. Until then, there's nothing to install.

What you'll need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
  • A Google account for Google Calendar, Google Tasks and Gmail, if you use them.
  • Apple Reminders works through macOS, with your permission.

Already invited?

Follow the install steps for beta testers, using the download link from your invite email.

Your email

We use your email only for early access and major DayBoard news. It's stored by DayBoard's own signup service and never sold or shared. To be removed at any time, email the DayBoard team. Details are in the privacy policy.
