Bring your own AI.

You still bring your own AI: Claude Code, Codex, or an API key from your provider. DayBoard Pro doesn't include AI usage. Your provider handles that under its own terms.

A few good things to know.

One payment. $5 once, with no subscription.
Future updates included. Pro keeps working as DayBoard updates.
Up to 3 Macs. To move your license, open Settings → License on the old Mac and choose Deactivate this device.
Try AI first. You get 7 days of AI free, starting the first time you use an AI feature. When the trial ends, only AI locks; everything else keeps working.
Beta testers get Pro free. Thank you for testing DayBoard.
14-day refunds. Not for you? Get a full refund within 14 days. See the refund policy.

Paying

Checkout is run by Stripe. Link, Stripe's checkout service, is the seller of record: it processes your payment, calculates any sales tax or VAT, and emails your receipt. Your statement shows LINK.COM* DAYBOARD. DayBoard never sees your card number. Your license key appears on the receipt page after you pay. See the privacy policy for what each side keeps.

Open source

DayBoard's source is licensed under GPL-3.0, so you can always build it yourself. Buying Pro funds development and doesn't change your rights under the license.
